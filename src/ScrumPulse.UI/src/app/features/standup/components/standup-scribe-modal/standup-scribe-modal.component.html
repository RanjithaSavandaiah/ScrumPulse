Capture the call while your squad speaks. The updates are written to the standup feed for you.

Before you start

This records the meeting audio and sends it for transcription. Tell your squad you are recording, and give anyone who would rather not be recorded the chance to send their update in writing instead.

The audio is transcribed and discarded, never stored. The updates are saved automatically once the standup ends, and you can edit any of them afterwards from the standup feed.

Sharing the right audio

Your browser will ask what to share. Choose Entire Screen and tick "Also share system audio" at the bottom of the dialog.

Do not pick Window. Chrome and Edge cannot capture audio from a single window, so sharing the Teams window records the picture and silence. Entire Screen works whether Teams is the desktop app or a browser tab.

You will also be asked for microphone access. System audio carries the other participants but not you, so the microphone is what puts your own update in the transcript.
