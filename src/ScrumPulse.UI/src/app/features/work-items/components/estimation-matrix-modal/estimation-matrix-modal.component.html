Sprint Commitment Formula

Committed SP = (Net Team Hours × 0.70) ÷ 8.0 hrs/pt

Deduct planned leaves from gross capacity, multiply by 70% productive focus factor (accounting for standups, reviews & testing), and divide by team hour/point benchmark.
Why Points ≠ Raw Hours

Story points capture relative size, complexity, technical risk, and uncertainty. A senior and junior developer may take different hours on the same task, but both agree on its relative 3 SP size.

The 13+ Story Point Rule

Any user story estimated at 13 or more story points carries high delivery volatility and must be sliced into smaller independent deliverables (2, 3, or 5 SP) before entering a sprint.
