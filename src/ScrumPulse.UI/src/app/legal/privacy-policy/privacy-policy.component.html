01

Information We Collect

When using ScrumPulse, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Sprint & Work Item Data: Agile tickets, story points, cycle times, PR review timestamps, and blocker descriptions submitted by your squads to power the velocity analytics engines.
  • Technical & Device Data: IP address, browser type, operating system version, referring URLs, device identifiers, and page latency metrics to guarantee platform uptime and security.
  • Role & Session Data: Selected agile roles (Scrum Master, Developer, QA, CDL, Agile Coach) stored locally in browser session storage for seamless tenant navigation.
02

How We Use Your Information

We process collected information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To generate real-time sprint burndown projections, blocker SLA warnings, and cycle-time benchmarks.
  • To empower the Microsoft AI Agile Coach with context-aware sprint guidance and blocker triage advice.
  • To defend against denial-of-service, automated scraping, and unauthorized system intrusion via sliding-window rate limiting.
  • To display non-intrusive, relevant advertisements through authorized networks to support free public hosting.
03

Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

Crucial AdSense Disclosure

ScrumPulse partners with Google AdSense to display advertisements across our public platform. This advertising revenue enables us to provide agile engineering teams worldwide with free access to high-velocity tools, burndown analytics, and AI coaching.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. Google uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to visitors based upon their visit to scrumpulse.onrender.com and other websites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its certified ad partners to serve ads to our users based on their prior visits to ScrumPulse and other internet websites.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's ad settings.

Third-Party Ad Networks & Web Beacons

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on ScrumPulse, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see.

Please note that ScrumPulse has no direct access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

Opting Out of Personalized Advertising

You can manage and opt out of interest-based advertising at any time through the following official industry resources:

Google Ads Settings (Ads Personalization) Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) WebChoices Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) Consumer Opt-Out
04

AI Coaching & Large Language Models

ScrumPulse incorporates an intelligent assistant powered by Microsoft AI agent architecture and LLM reasoning. Interactions with the AI Coach are ephemeral and focused strictly on the sprint context provided. We do not use your private enterprise backlog data or internal sprint metrics to train foundational public LLM weights.

05

Cookies & Local Browser Storage

We use minimal, privacy-centric browser storage mechanisms:

  • Essential Storage: Local storage is utilized to persist dark/light theme preferences and squad filters across browser sessions without requiring personal profile logins.
  • Advertising Cookies: Placed by Google AdSense as outlined in Section 3 above.
06

Security & Data Protection

ScrumPulse implements OWASP-recommended security practices, including strict Content Security Policies (CSP), HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS), and sliding-window rate limiting to protect all users and service infrastructure from malicious requests.

07

Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global Inquiries)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have specific rights concerning your personal data:

  • Right to Know & Access: The right to request confirmation of whether your data is being processed and obtain a copy.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: The right to request deletion of any telemetry data collected during your session.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing: ScrumPulse does not sell personal data. You may opt out of third-party cookie sharing via the DAA and Google Ad Settings links above.
